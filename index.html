About Your Instructor

In 2017, I joined the USCCA because I wanted to do everything I could to protect my family and friends. What started as a passion for shooting quickly grew into a calling — I felt urged to become an instructor. I loved the USCCA program so much that I earned one certification, then another, and another. My goal is to achieve every certification they offer, so I can be the best instructor not only for my family but for every student who takes my class. When you leave training at Broussard Shooting Academy, my promise is that you’ll feel it was time and money well spent.

Certified to Protect & Educate
