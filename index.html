About Your Instructor

In 2017, I joined the USCCA because I wanted to do everything I could to protect my family and friends. What started as a passion for shooting quickly grew into a calling — I felt urged to become an instructor. I loved the USCCA program so much that I earned one certification, then another, and another. My goal is to achieve every certification they offer, so I can be the best instructor not only for my family but for every student who takes my class. When you leave training at Broussard Shooting Academy, my promise is that you’ll feel it was time and money well spent.

Courses & Pricing

Find the course that fits your skill level. All taught by certified instructor David Broussard.

CourseDescriptionPrice
Concealed Carry This full certification course covers firearm safety, Louisiana law, home defense, and live-fire training. Students gain the confidence to carry responsibly, understand their legal rights and limits, and build essential decision-making skills. Our goal is to make sure you leave fully prepared and safe. $125
Permitless Carry A focused mini-course that explains Louisiana’s permitless carry law. Learn where you can and cannot carry, your responsibilities, and how to avoid legal pitfalls. Perfect for anyone who carries without a permit but still values safety and compliance. $50
First Aid This course teaches critical trauma response, including bleeding control and emergency first steps. You’ll gain practical skills that save lives in everyday emergencies, at home, or at the range. Confidence under pressure is the goal. $100
Church Safety Designed for staff and volunteers, this course addresses team communication, threat recognition, and emergency planning in a church setting. It helps build a respectful, faith-based safety culture. Protect your congregation with knowledge and preparation. $100
Shooter Threat Learn how to recognize warning signs and respond effectively to active threats. Students train in realistic scenarios to practice awareness, stress responses, and defensive options. This training empowers you to act responsibly when seconds matter. $100
Agent Safety Tailored to real estate professionals, this course emphasizes situational awareness and safe practices during showings and client meetings. Learn defensive options and protocols to reduce risks in a vulnerable profession. Practical, professional, and effective. $50
Children’s Firearms Safety A family-friendly program that teaches parents and children about safe storage, rules for firearm encounters, and age-appropriate handling. Kids learn responsibility, while parents gain peace of mind. Build family confidence together. $75
General Pistol A fundamentals class covering grip, stance, sight alignment, and trigger control. Students practice safe pistol operation in a supportive environment. Great for beginners and those seeking a refresher on shooting basics. $40

* Live Fire held at Neutral Ground Gun Company. Sign Waiver.
