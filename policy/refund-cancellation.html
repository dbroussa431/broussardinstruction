Refund & Cancellation Policy

At Broussard Shooting Academy, we are committed to fairness, professionalism, and safety. Our refund and cancellation policy ensures classes run smoothly while protecting student flexibility.

Student Cancellations

  • 72+ hours before class: Full refund minus a $10 processing fee, or free reschedule.
  • Within 72 hours of class: One free reschedule. Refunds (minus the $10 fee) may be considered case-by-case depending on seat availability.
  • No-shows: Not eligible for a refund. Please contact us to discuss options for rescheduling.

Rescheduling

  • Students may request one free reschedule with at least 24 hours’ notice.
  • Requests made with less than 24 hours’ notice may require new registration depending on class capacity.

Academy-Initiated Changes

If Broussard Shooting Academy must postpone or cancel a class (e.g., weather, facility issues, instructor availability), students may choose between a full refund or a free transfer to a future date. No processing fee will apply.

How to Request

Please provide your name, class, and scheduled date when requesting a refund or reschedule:

  • Email: BroussardShootingAcademy@protonmail.com
  • Phone: 504-289-6605

We appreciate your trust. These policies help us maintain safe, high-quality firearm education while being fair to every student.
