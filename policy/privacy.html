Privacy Policy

Broussard Shooting Academy respects your privacy and collects only the information necessary to provide high-quality training and support for our students.

Information We Collect

  • Personal information such as your name, email address, and phone number when you register or inquire about courses.
  • Analytics data via Google Analytics to understand website traffic and improve services. This may involve cookies or similar technologies.

How We Use Information

  • To communicate with you about classes, scheduling, and support.
  • To maintain secure student records and certifications.
  • To improve our website and outreach using aggregated, non-personal data.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Data is shared only with trusted service providers (e.g., analytics platforms, payment processors) as needed to deliver services.

Your Choices

  • You may disable cookies in your browser settings.
  • You may opt out of Google Analytics using Google’s tools.
  • You may request access to or deletion of your personal information by contacting us.

Contact

For privacy questions, email BroussardShootingAcademy@protonmail.com or call 504-289-6605.

We are committed to protecting your data and building trust with every student.
