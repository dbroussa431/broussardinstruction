Broussard Shooting Academy Standard

Before You Continue

Read and acknowledge the standard you are training to.

At Broussard Shooting Academy, we train for one purpose:

To go home safely.

  • Avoid danger whenever possible.
  • Do not go where problems happen.
  • Do not stay where problems are developing.

If you are not there, you cannot become part of the problem.

Just because you have it does not mean you use it.

With great power comes great responsibility.
