Lesson 8 - Firearm Safety Foundations

In Progress

The first lesson covers safe handling, muzzle discipline, trigger discipline, and the responsibility that comes with using any firearm.

Students should understand that every firearm must be treated as if it is loaded, that the muzzle should never point at anything they are not willing to destroy, and that their finger stays off the trigger until they are ready to fire.

This lesson also introduces range awareness, safe storage thinking, and the mindset needed before ever stepping onto a live firing line.
