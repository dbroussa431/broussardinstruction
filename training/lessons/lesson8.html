8. Louisiana Law and Mental Health

This lesson covers Louisiana-specific firearm law, permitless carry updates, legal responsibilities, prohibited locations, and the critical role of mental health awareness, suicide prevention, and responsible firearm storage.

Louisiana Carry Law (2024 Update)

  • Permitless concealed carry is allowed for eligible individuals 18+.
  • All legal restrictions still apply even without a permit.
  • Prohibited persons include felons, unlawful drug users, and certain mental health adjudications.

Where You CANNOT Carry

  • Courthouses, law enforcement buildings, and detention facilities.
  • Schools, polling places, and government buildings.
  • Bars and establishments serving alcohol.
  • Private property where firearms are restricted.

Legal Responsibility While Carrying

  • You must notify law enforcement when approached.
  • Carrying under the influence is illegal.
  • You are responsible for all actions while armed.

Mental Health and Responsibility

  • Most firearm deaths are suicides, not homicides.
  • Crises are often temporary but decisions can be permanent.
  • Creating time and distance saves lives.
  • Safe storage is critical during high-risk periods.

Decision-Making Under Stress

  • Fast decisions are not always correct decisions.
  • Judgment matters more than speed.
  • Escalation has consequences.
  • The safest decision is often to disengage.
Important: You must understand BOTH legal requirements and responsible decision-making to pass.
